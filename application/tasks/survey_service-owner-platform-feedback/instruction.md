# Field-Service Platform Feedback Survey

We're asking owners and operators of trade and home-service businesses
(plumbing, HVAC, electrical, drain, appliance repair and similar), from single
shops to companies with hundreds of employees, to react to a field-service
platform that runs booking, dispatch, field work and invoicing.

Read the product brief in the task context, then complete every required
question. Answer from your own business, background, budget and habits.

## How to answer

- Read the brief before you start. It separates what is built today from what
  is not built yet.
- The first question asks how you relate to service businesses. Answer it
  honestly. If you don't run one, answer the rest as you would if you were
  advising a service business of your own company's size that you know well.
- Judge features by what they would do for your revenue, your customers'
  experience and your team's day, not by how impressive they sound.
- Answer every required question.
- Use the exact choice ids for single- and multi-choice questions.
- For multi-choice questions, select every id that applies.
- For rating scales, use a whole number in the given range.
- Give the answer alone unless a question also asks for a short reason.

The platform records your answers; do not invent submission file paths.
