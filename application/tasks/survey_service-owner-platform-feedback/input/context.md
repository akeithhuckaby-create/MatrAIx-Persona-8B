# Field-service platform: product brief

**Category:** Software for trade and home-service companies, from single
shops to large operators with hundreds of employees
**Stage:** Working first release, run by one pilot shop before wider launch.
The screens shown in demos use a fictional sample shop, Demo Plumbing Co.

## One-liner

Customers book online and see a real, all-in price before anyone drives out.
The same system runs the office, dispatch, the technician's phone and the invoice.

## Built today

1. **Online booking with an instant price.** A widget on the shop's own website
   asks the customer a few triage questions about the problem. It returns a
   firm price, a price range, a paid diagnostic visit, or "call us now" for
   emergencies. Every shown price already includes mandatory fees such as the
   trip fee. Customers pick a real open arrival window, add photos, and record
   their text, email and call consent. Out-of-area ZIP codes are turned away
   with the shop's phone number. The shop's license number shows on the page.
2. **Bookings inbox and desk dashboard.** Each online booking arrives once with
   the customer, property, triage answers, quoted price and photos, already
   holding a technician and window. The office confirms it or declines it with
   a reason. The desk shows bookings waiting, unassigned jobs, today's jobs,
   unpaid invoices and how far final invoices drift from the original quote.
3. **Dispatch board and week capacity view.** Technician rows show shifts, time
   off and jobs by hour. The office drags jobs onto free time and double
   bookings are refused. A call-taker can find a caller by name, phone or
   street, see their history and book a job into an open window.
4. **Technician phone app that works with no signal.** Jobs and briefs,
   required checklists, before and after photos, and the customer's signature
   at completion. Work done offline syncs when signal returns.
5. **Good / better / best estimates with e-sign.** On site, the technician
   builds up to three options from the shop's price list and the customer signs
   on the phone.
6. **Invoicing from the job.** The invoice starts from the quoted service. The
   office or the technician adds items, discounts and tax, then emails it.
7. **Timesheets.** Technicians clock in, take breaks and clock out on the phone,
   and job taps record drive and work time. The office fixes mistakes with a
   reason and exports hours for payroll.
8. **Equipment history.** Each property lists installed equipment with install
   dates and service history.
9. **Install crews, routing and automatic dispatch.** Sold replacements get
   install dates from permit, equipment and crew availability, and crews are
   scheduled across several days. Each technician's day can be re-routed to
   cut drive time. Automatic dispatch ranks technicians by average ticket and
   closing rate and steers high-value jobs to the best closers.
10. **Roles and shop separation.** Owners, office staff and technicians each
    see only their own screens. An owner can switch between shops, and one
    shop can never see another shop's data.

Setup is direct entry. The owner types services, prices and appointment
lengths into a price list and publishes versions. No import file is required.

## Not built yet

- Card and ACH payments (invoices are emailed but not paid in the app)
- QuickBooks sync
- Customer texts (confirmation, reminder, on-my-way)
- Online bookings creating scheduled jobs on their own
- Memberships and maintenance reminders
- Reports beyond the desk dashboard
- A customer portal beyond viewing their own service details
- Importing customers, equipment and history from another system
- Multi-location and franchise management (branches rolled up under one company)
- A public API and webhooks for connecting other systems
- SOC 2 security certification
